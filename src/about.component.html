Rangular Tutorial

Version 1.0.0 · 100 lessons

A step by step tutorial that teaches the Rangular framework: writing iOS and Android apps the Angular way. Every screen in this app, including this one, started as Angular code and was compiled by Rangular into React Native.

Lessons follow the official Angular documentation, topic by topic. Each topic gets its Rangular equivalent: a short explanation, a Code block, a live demo, and a best practice.

Your saved lessons, your recent lessons, and your progress are kept on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere. They keep across restarts, so the lesson you were reading is still your place when you come back.

There is no account and no sign in. The app asks for nothing about you, and there is nothing to leak.

Rangular Framework is Bigyan Chapagain's project on GitHub, and Rangular Tutorial is his own dogfooding project, tutorial, and documentation all put together.

The Rangular framework is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. This app is its tutorial and its test at the same time: if a lesson teaches it, this app uses it.

This app is being prepared for release on the App Store and Google Play. All 100 lessons are complete, and the icon, splash artwork, and store listing are prepared. The store accounts and the submission itself are the remaining steps.
